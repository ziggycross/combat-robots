Who We Are

Build it.
Break it.
Repeat.

Vancouver Combat Robots is a community-run league for builders, engineers, and competitors who want to put their creations to the ultimate test. We welcome everyone from first-time builders to seasoned veterans.

Robots are built, repaired, and improved between events — the pit is just as important as the arena.

Weight Classes

Pick Your Division

1lb

Antweight

The nimblest weight class in our league. Antweights pack serious aggression into a tiny frame. Perfect for first-time builders — cheaper to build, faster to repair.

  • Max Weight: 1 lb
  • Arena: Shared full arena
  • Format: Round robin + finals
  • Entry: Free — donations welcome
3lb

Beetleweight

The flagship class of Vancouver Combat Robots. More room for complex drivetrains, weapons, and armour. Beetles hit hard and the battles show it.

  • Max Weight: 3 lb
  • Arena: Shared full arena
  • Format: Round robin + finals
  • Entry: Free — donations welcome

Not sure which class to enter? Come watch an event first — everyone's welcome to spectate for free.
